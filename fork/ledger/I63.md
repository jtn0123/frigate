| I63 | CI: Sonar keeps browser coverage when an e2e shard fails | `.github/workflows/fork-checks.yml` (`sonar` job), `fork/SONAR-CI.md` | The `sonar` job merged Playwright coverage only when `web_e2e` succeeded. On 2026-10-01 one flaky retry on a push to `next` (strict flaky report) failed shard 3, so all browser coverage was dropped and the gate also failed on new-code coverage (78.1%) and overall coverage fell from 52% to 39%. The job now merges browser coverage whenever all three shards uploaded it; the shard failure still fails "Checks passed" on its own | fork |
