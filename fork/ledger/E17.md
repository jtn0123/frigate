| E17 | security: zone rename | hunks in `web/src/lib/fork/zone-rename.ts` (`isUnsafeKey`, `setPath`, `mergeInto`); cases in `web/src/lib/fork/zone-rename.test.ts` | `setPath` and `mergeInto` build the `config/set` body of a zone rename from a query string, zone names and a caller's object, and wrote through any key they were given (CodeQL `js/prototype-pollution-utility`, alert #51). A path with a `__proto__`, `constructor` or `prototype` segment, or a parsed JSON body with `__proto__` as an own key, reached `Object.prototype`. `setPath` now drops the whole write when any segment is one of the three, and `mergeInto` skips those keys; the comparisons are spelled out next to each assignment, the form CodeQL reads as a guard. The tests pollute nothing through `queryToConfigData`, `zoneRename` or `zoneRenameConfigData` and keep the safe keys of the same input.
