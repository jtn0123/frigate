| I162 | CI: audio companion layer cache | `.github/workflows/fork-checks.yml` (`audio_companion` job), `fork/scripts/test_fork_workflows.py` | The job ran a plain `docker build` of `fork/audio_trial`, whose apt step reads one host, `snapshot.debian.org`, at a fixed timestamp. That host was down for hours on 2026-11-05 and 06, and about six runs failed or timed out, blocking `next`'s "Checks passed". The image is now built with `docker/build-push-action` (pinned by commit, like `setup-buildx-action`) from the Actions cache (`type=gha`, scope `audio-companion`, `mode=max`) and loaded as `frigate-audio-check`, so apt and pip only reach the network when the Dockerfile or a file it copies changes. Pull requests read the cache that pushes to `next` save, and a failed cache save only warns. The digest-pinned base, the fixed snapshot and the hash-pinned lock mean a cached layer holds the packages a fresh build would install, and every check after the build still runs against the real image; no timeout, retry or skip changed. The new test keeps third-party actions in the fork's workflows pinned to a commit and the job on its cache and its checks. The other images "Fork - Checks" builds read `deb.debian.org`, a CDN with many backends, so they keep their plain builds | fork-only |
