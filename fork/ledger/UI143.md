| UI143 | recordings: synced multi-camera playback | `web/src/views/fork/SyncedPlaybackView.tsx`, `web/src/lib/fork/synced-playback.ts`, `web/src/lib/fork/synced-playback-engine.ts`, `web/src/hooks/fork/use-synced-playback.ts`, `web/src/hooks/fork/use-synced-playback-mode.ts`, `web/src/components/fork/SyncedPlaybackToggle.tsx`, a toggle and slot hunk in `web/src/views/recording/RecordingView.tsx`, the `syncedPlayback` flag, `fork.json` strings, unit tests and `web/e2e/specs/fork/synced-playback.spec.ts` | A "Multi-camera" toggle in the recording view plays 2 to 4 full-resolution cameras (2 on phones) on one master clock that drives the timeline, play and pause, seek, step and speed. The default is the main camera plus the cameras with activity nearest the playhead, and a picker adds or removes cameras. Two cameras sit side by side on a desktop unless stacking gives clearly larger tiles. A camera with no footage at the master time shows "No recording" with its next start while the others keep playing, and at the end of the saved footage it shows "Caught up to live" with a Go to Live button. Drift is corrected by rate nudges and hard seeks, a click promotes a tile to main without moving any tile and a double-click shows it fullscreen. The grid's speeds stop at 8x (a 16x choice from the single player plays at 8x), non-main tiles prefer the sub stream, and tile buttons are 44 px on touch screens. Gated by `syncedPlayback`. | fork-only |
