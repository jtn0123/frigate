| UI134 | web: one search entry in the rail, and it searches footage | new `web/src/components/fork/NavSearchButton.tsx`, `web/src/lib/fork/nav-search.ts` (+ `.test.ts`), `web/src/hooks/fork/use-footage-search.ts` (+ `.test.tsx`), `web/e2e/specs/fork/rail-search.spec.ts`; hunks in `web/src/components/fork/CommandPalette.tsx`, `web/src/components/fork/ForkNavItems.tsx`, `web/src/components/navigation/Sidebar.tsx`, `web/public/locales/en/fork.json`, `web/e2e/pages/base.page.ts`, `web/e2e/specs/navigation.spec.ts`, `web/e2e/specs/fork/command-palette.spec.ts` | The desktop rail carried two ways to search: a magnifier that jumped to Explore and a `⌘` button that opened the palette, so the one icon that means "search" did not open the search box. They are one button now. The rail's magnifier opens the palette, the `⌘` button is gone from the sidebar (the phone still opens the palette from the Settings drawer, and a tablet's bottom bar keeps its button), and the palette looks through recorded footage as well as pages, cameras, settings and actions. Footage rows come from `/events/search`, debounced 300 ms so a typed word costs one request, capped at five with a "see all" row that hands the query to `/explore?query=`; a row opens that clip at `/explore?event_id=`. That endpoint is the semantic one and answers 400 when semantic search is off, so the request is gated on `semantic_search.enabled` rather than left to fail into a read-error toast: without it, an admin sees one quiet row at the end of the list pointing at the setting. The footage group is force mounted and sits above the command matches, because a clip of a car does not contain the words "red car" and cmdk's fuzzy filter would drop every row; force mounting also keeps them out of the automatic selection, so Enter still runs the top command match. Losing the rail's Explore link costs the desktop page object its link, so `BasePage.navigateTo` falls back to the search box for any route the rail no longer links to. 6 vitest cases and 6 e2e specs across desktop and phone.
